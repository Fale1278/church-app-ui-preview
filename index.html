Install TEAM International

Enjoy full-screen view, fast offline access, and instant launching directly from your phone or desktop home screen.

Native Installation
Browser Instructions
iOS (iPhone / iPad Safari)
1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari.
2. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
3. Tap Add at the top right.
Android (Chrome / Edge / Firefox)
1. Tap the ⋮ menu icon at the top right.
2. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
3. Confirm installation.
Desktop (Chrome / Edge / Brave)
Look for the install icon (⊕ or 💻) on the right side of your browser address bar and click Install.
NOTE: PWA installation requires hosting over HTTPS or localhost server.
